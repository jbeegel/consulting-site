## KQ1. How big and fast-growing is Owala; what is Owala's stance on licensed/collegiate products; is the Fanatic Group exclusive public?

### Takeaway
Owala (Trove Brands, Lehi UT, launched 2020) is now the most-searched drinkware brand in the US after Yeti (1.22M monthly Google searches for "owala" vs 823K "yeti", 673K "stanley cup"), with Google Trends interest ~27x the 5-year level of "stanley cup tumbler" and still rising into Nov 2026. Revenue is undisclosed (est. $250–500M). The collegiate program is real and already in market: The Fanatic Group's Spring 2026 catalog announces a "new drinkware partnership with Owala for 2026" and school-logo FreeSip 24oz bottles sell at $44.99–$50 through TFG's own site and campus stores, but there is no Owala/Trove press release, no Owala-site mention, and essentially no organic search visibility yet.

### Inferences
- Licensed collegiate Owala carries a ~$14–20 (45–65%) premium over the plain 24oz FreeSip ($44.99–$50 vs ~$30), in line with Yeti's collegiate premium ($50 for a 20oz Rambler vs ~$35–38 plain) and well above Tervis's.
- The exclusive is effectively "soft-launched": distribution exists via campus bookstores and TFG's own Shopify site, but there is zero Owala-side amplification and (see KQ2) near-zero organic search footprint, so most Owala fans do not know collegiate versions exist.

- The category is cooling overall (Circana) while Owala takes share; a collegiate line is a share-of-Owala-wallet play, not a bet on category growth.

### Inferences
- Collegiate Owala demand is being *created* by retail availability rather than pre-existing: school terms with TFG product in store (Tennessee, Ohio State, WVU, Iowa, Oregon) show the fall 2026 inflections; schools without visible product show flat lines. The sum of school+Owala searches (~1,600/mo in Sep–Oct 2026) is already approaching the Yeti long-tail per-school numbers of two years ago and is growing from a near-zero base.
- The commercially richest adjacent intent is "custom/customized/branded Owala" (~20,000+ combined monthly searches at $5–$21 CPCs) — group/Greek/department/corporate buyers — which TFG's custom-branded-gift capability can serve.
- "stanley collegiate" (210/mo, LOW competition) with no Stanley collegiate program evidences unmet demand for a hype-brand collegiate bottle that Owala can capture.
- Expect ~3–5x December and ~2–3x Sep–Oct seasonality on school-branded drinkware terms, based on Yeti/Stanley/generic school-tumbler series.
- SEO is wide open: for all but Tennessee, no collegiate-Owala page ranks; school-level landing pages titled "[School] Owala FreeSip" would face little competition.

## KQ8. Ideal online catalog structure for collegiate Owala, with examples
