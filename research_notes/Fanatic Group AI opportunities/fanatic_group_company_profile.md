## Q1. Who runs it, where is it based, how big is it, when was it founded, corporate history and related entities?

### Takeaway
The Fanatic Group, LLC is a small (roughly 10–25 person, <$5M revenue per directory estimates), family-run, Edison NJ licensed-drinkware/gift printer led by President Gregg Solomon. It is one arm of the Solomon family's "Consortium Companies" printing group (Prodigital Printing, Printmarx, MGF) sharing a 30,000 sq ft Raritan Center facility and the same phone number; it was started around 2010 (LLC records say 2005) as a licensed collegiate paper-products business and pivoted to full-color digitally printed drinkware, launching a Shopify D2C store in late 2020.
